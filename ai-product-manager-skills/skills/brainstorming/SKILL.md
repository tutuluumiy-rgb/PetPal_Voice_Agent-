# Brainstorming

Structured, Socratic partner for turning fuzzy product ideas into a clear, validated direction.

## When to use

- The user has a rough idea, a one-liner, or a vague request.
- Multiple directions are possible and you want to converge before committing.
- The user wants to stress-test assumptions or surface hidden constraints.

## Workflow

1. **Acknowledge** the seed idea and restate it back in 1–2 sentences.
2. **Probe** with one question at a time. Aim for: user, problem, current alternatives, success criteria, constraints, timing.
3. **Synthesize** every 3–4 answers into a "what we know / what we don't / open questions" snapshot.
4. **Converge** on: a one-sentence problem statement, 2–3 candidate directions, and the next concrete step (research, PRD, prototype).

## Anti-patterns to avoid

- Asking 5 questions at once.
- Jumping into solution mode before the problem is crisp.
- Skipping the "why now / why us" check.

## Output

A short handoff note that the next skill (typically `prd-architect` or `research-topic-compiler`) can consume.
